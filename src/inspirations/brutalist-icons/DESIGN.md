# Brutalist icon design system

There is no external source URL. Evan's source direction is the original August 24, 2026 request for an Instinct icon with an "altered aesthetic like digital brutalist" plus the resulting one-bit Xerox and cut-concrete visual treatments. Do not attribute this to an invented website.

## Tokens
Paper `#ebe8de`, ink `#111`, orange `#ef4e2d`, concrete `#aaa69b`, dark concrete `#171715`. Arial Black for display, Arial for body, monospace for labels. 4px hard borders, zero-radius major panels, 7-9px offset shadows, 24px icon geometry. Mobile uses two-column specimens; desktop seven.

## Product and marketing patterns
Marketing is loud uppercase editorial brutalism with hard divisions. Product is a searchable Lucide specimen browser, one-bit Xerox and cut-concrete material modes, grid/list controls, detail/code view, and related objects. Lucide remains the swappable source primitive; degradation stays in CSS.
